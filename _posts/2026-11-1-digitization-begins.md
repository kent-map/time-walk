Deal and Sandwich museums have focussed on under-utilized postcard collections as part of their digitisation programme. At Sandwich volunteers Lauren, Paige, Anna and Rosie have catalogued and digitized thirty-six postcards of churches and other public buildings such as The Barbican which you can see [here](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=%22Let%27s+do+the+time+walk+again%22+sandwich&title=Special%3AMediaSearch&type=image). Now the story-telling and mapping phase begins as the volunteers think about how they want to use these postcards to tell the story of Sandwich.

In Deal, volunteers Stuart, Grace, Adam, Charlotte and Rosie have digitized twenty-nine postcards which focus on the beach and Deal's maritime past which you can see [here](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=%22Let%27s+do+the+time+walk+again%22+deal&title=Special%3AMediaSearch&type=image). With a theme of On the Beach, volunteers chose a range of postcards which reflected the diverse activities that take place along the coast – from sprat fishing, life-boat rescues, shipwrecks, to crazy golf and other seaside leisure activities. Taking an image each, volunteers began searching the British Newspaper Archive via the museum’s Findmypast subscription. Thinking carefully about keywords – Deal having more than one meaning– the volunteers found articles in both national and local newspapers that provided colour and context to articles they were writing. Sprat fishing for example suffered peaks and troughs with bad weather, abundancy of fish stocks, and low wages all impacting the industry.
